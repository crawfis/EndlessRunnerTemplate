Art replaces greybox when the metrics are locked and the loop is fun, not when someone gets bored of cubes.

  • Art respects the greybox numbers. Jump height, reach, gap width, character scale — the greybox decided those, and the model conforms to them.
  • If art forces a metric to change, that is a design decision, not an art decision: make it deliberately and re-playtest.
  • One system at a time. Replace the player, ship, capture, playtest. Then the environment. Replacing everything at once means you cannot tell which change made it worse.
  • Keep a greybox scene alive for testing and for the before/after comparisons.
  • Never art something you might cut. Check the ranking first.
