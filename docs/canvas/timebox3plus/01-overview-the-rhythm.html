CSE 5912 Capstone · Every timebox from here

This document covers Timebox 3 and every timebox after it. The rhythm does not change; only the goal does. Each timebox: pick one sentence's worth of goal, build it, put it in front of players, ship a build someone else can run, and show what changed.

Three things are different from Timebox 2:

Timebox 2 Timebox 3 onward
Design generate the whole backlog from nothing re-rank the backlog you have, one timebox ahead
Build about half your hours about two thirds
New work — capture, before/after, a video diary, and light marketing

That last row is the addition, and it is small: a couple of hours per timebox, owned by one person. It exists because the record of how your game changed is worth as much as the change itself — for your final presentation, your postmortem, and anyone you ever want to show this to.

Everything else carries forward unchanged from Timebox 2: the definition of done, the review ring, the 48-hour freeze, the estimates-versus-actuals discipline, the agent contract, and the retro that produces three actions with owners.
